# About and credits

**WHDLoadSuite** runs WHDLoad game archives in the Amiberry emulator and turns them into standalone programs. It was written by DimFil in 2026. **Help > About WHDLoadSuite…** shows the version.

## Credits

- **Retroplay** on the English Amiga Board (EAB) and everyone who builds WHDLoad packs. The Downloader only browses and downloads their work. All credit goes to them. The EAB forum thread: https://eab.abime.net/showthread.php?t=61028
- **Amiberry**: https://github.com/BlitterStudio/amiberry
- **WHDLoad**, by Wepl (whdload.de), and everyone who writes WHDLoad installs.
- **GamebaseAmiga**: the pictures of the launch screen come from the GameBase Amiga collection. All credit goes to that project and its contributors.
- **The Unarchiver** (`unar`): used to unpack the Gamebase pictures.
