# Folders, emulator and Kickstarts

Three things must be in the right place before games will run: the games themselves, the emulator, and the Kickstart ROMs. This page is the quick overview; the pages below it explain everything in detail.

- [Folder locations](help:locations): every folder the app uses, what it holds, and how to change it.
- [The emulator (Amiberry)](help:emulator): where Amiberry must be, how the app finds it, and the app's own copy of its settings.
- [Kickstart ROMs](help:kickstarts): what they are, which ones you need, where they go, and how to check that Amiberry accepts them.

## At a glance

- **Your `.lha` game archives** go in the **downloads folder** (`work/downloads` by default). Change it in **Settings > Browser…**.
- **Amiberry** goes in the `emulator` folder next to the app (`emulator/Amiberry.app` on macOS, an `amiberry` folder on Windows and Linux), or anywhere you choose in **Settings > Amiberry…**.
- **Kickstart ROMs** go in `emulator/Kickstarts`. Files must end in `.rom`.
- **Standalone programs you build** are saved in the **Executables** folder (`work/Executables` by default).
- **The app's own data**, such as its private copy of Amiberry's settings, is kept in your user folder, apart from your own Amiberry. See [Folder locations](help:locations).

> **Tip:** **Settings > Amiberry…** shows whether Amiberry was found and which of your Kickstart ROMs it recognised. If something does not start, look there first.
