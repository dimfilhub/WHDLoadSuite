# Browser settings

**Settings > Browser…** has one setting.

## What you see

- **Downloads folder:** the path, a box you can type in, and a **Browse…** button, with a note under it.

## What you can do

- Press **Browse…** and choose the folder where your WHDLoad `.lha` archives are kept, or type the path.

## What it changes

This is the **shared downloads folder**:

- The [Browser](help:browser) lists it.
- The [Builder](help:builder) builds from it.
- The [Downloader](help:downloader) saves into it (changing one changes the other).
- [HDCommander](help:hdcommander) starts its host pane in it.

All tabs refresh as soon as you change it. The folder may contain sub-folders; the app looks inside them too.

## Tips

- The default is `work/downloads` next to the app. See [Folder locations](help:locations).
