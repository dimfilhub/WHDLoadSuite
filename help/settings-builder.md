# Builder settings

**Settings > Builder…** has three folder settings.

## What you see

Three rows, each with a path, a **Browse…** button and a note:

- **Work folder:** the folder that holds `Template_WHDLoad` and `Icons`.
- **Prepared (Whdload) folder:** where games that need a [prepared drive](help:prepared) get theirs.
- **App folder** (macOS) or **Exe folder** (Windows and Linux): where **Build App** or **Build Exe** saves the standalone programs.

## What you can do

- Choose a different folder with **Browse…**, or type a path.
- The prepared and executable folders **follow the work folder** while they are still at their defaults (`Whdload` and `Executables` inside it). Once you choose another folder for one of them, it stays where you put it.

## What it changes

- The Builder and the Browser re-read which games are prepared and built, so the colours update.
- Games already prepared or built in the old folders are not moved.

## Tips

- See [Folder locations](help:locations) for what each folder holds, and [Standalone executables](help:executables) for the build.
